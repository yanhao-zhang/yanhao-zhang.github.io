# About Me

I am Yanhao Zhang (张严浩), Ph.D., Algorithm Expert, a member of the Cognitive and Interactive Vision & Digital Intelligence E-commerce team in the Machine Intelligence Technology Lab, Alibaba Damo Academy. My adviser is Prof. Rong Jin. I completed my Ph.D. at Harbin Institute of Technology with Prof. Qingming Huang.

My research interests include multimedia retrieval, social media analysis, and computer vision. I have worked on industrial projects including Pailitao and TaobaoLive, and on video content structuring and multi-modal learning.

Email: [zyhhog@163.com](mailto:zyhhog@163.com) · [WordPress](https://yanhaozhang.wordpress.com) · [Original homepage](https://sites.google.com/view/yanhao-zhang)
