# Yanhao Zhang

Hello! I am **Yanhao Zhang**. I currently lead the Multimodal Interaction Algorithm team at OPPO’s AI Center, where I have worked since July 2022, developing multimodal foundation models and AI agents for system-level AI products.

My research interests focus on **multimodal understanding and generation, AI agents, and on-device–cloud collaborative intelligence**, with a particular interest in how models perceive context, reason across modalities, and use tools to accomplish tasks in real-world environments.

My current work focuses on **building multimodal agentic systems for everyday interactions**, connecting model development with practical product needs. I work on cloud-based mixture-of-experts models and on-device agent models, with an emphasis on agent harnesses, reinforcement learning, and on-device–cloud coordination to improve end-to-end task success and response efficiency. These efforts support core experiences in OPPO’s Xiaobu Assistant, including contextual assistance, screen understanding, visual question answering, and image generation.

Before joining OPPO, I was an Algorithm Expert at Alibaba DAMO Academy, working on image retrieval and recognition, video understanding, and their applications across Taobao and Alibaba Cloud. I also conducted postdoctoral research through the Zhejiang University–Alibaba joint postdoctoral program. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Qingming Huang, and was a visiting scholar at Brown University. I have published more than 30 papers at leading international conferences, including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, and AAAI, and have filed more than 30 patent applications.
